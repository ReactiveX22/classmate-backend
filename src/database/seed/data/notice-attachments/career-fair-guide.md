Date: {{career_fair_date}}. Time: {{career_fair_time}}. Location: Student Center Exhibition Hall, 2nd Floor. Bring printed resumes. Dress is business casual.

| Booth | Employer | Roles |

## Resume review before the fair

Walk in review: {{resume_review_date}}, Career Services Office, Room 110, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Bring one printed draft. Staff will mark it up in about 10 minutes.

## Checklist for the day

Print 10 resumes on plain paper. Write your Starlight ID on the back of one copy for interview signup. Note three booths to visit first. Have a 30 second intro ready: name, major, year, one project, what role you want.

Bags go in the coat area. The hall has water stations at both ends. For questions, write to careers@starlight.edu.
