# Dean's Excellence Scholarship, Application Sheet

Award: {{scholarship_amount}} per semester, renewable for one year. For Spring {{next_semester_year}}. Deadline: {{scholarship_deadline}} at 11:59 PM through the Financial Aid portal.

## Who can apply

CSE major, 60 credit hours completed by Dec 31. GPA 3.5 or higher. Leadership in a campus activity or community service in the last 12 months.

## Papers to upload

1. Application form, signed PDF, from the portal.
2. Official transcript, issued within 30 days.
3. Two letters of recommendation. At least one from a Starlight instructor. Letters must be on letterhead and signed.
4. Personal statement, 500 words or less. State your major, one project, one service role, and how the funds help.

Files must be PDF, under 5 MB each. Name files: Lastname Form, Lastname Transcript, Lastname Letter1, Lastname Statement.

## How applications are scored

GPA and progress: 30 points. Statement: 30 points. Letters: 20 points. Service record: 20 points. Late or missing papers are not scored.

## Dates

Deadline: {{scholarship_deadline}}. Decisions: within 21 days. Funds post to student accounts before spring add drop ends.

Contact: dean.scholarships@starlight.edu. Office: Engineering Hall, Room 120.
