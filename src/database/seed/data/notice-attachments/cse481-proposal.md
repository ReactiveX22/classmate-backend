# CSE 481 Project Proposal Guidelines

Course: Natural Language Processing and LLMs. Instructor: Professor Jenkins. Original deadline was {{proposal_original}}. New deadline is {{proposal_deadline}} at 11:59 PM.

## Length and format

Two pages, PDF, 11 point font or larger. Put team members and Starlight IDs on page 1. Upload to the classroom portal under Assignments, CSE 481 Proposal. One upload per team.

## Required sections

1. Problem. Two or three sentences on what you will build and who it helps.
2. Data. Name the dataset, size, and license. If you collect data, say how many samples and how you will label them.
3. Method. Name the baseline model and one improvement you will test. List compute needs, for example GPU hours on the lab machines.
4. Evaluation. Name two metrics and how you will split train, dev, and test.
5. Schedule. Three milestones with dates before week 12.

## Grading

Scope and clarity: 40 points. Method and baseline: 30 points. Evaluation plan: 20 points. Schedule and roles: 10 points. Proposals under two full pages lose 10 points.

## Late rule

One day late loses 10 points. After two days the portal closes. If you already submitted before {{proposal_original}}, you do not need to resubmit.
