# Academic Integrity Policy, Use of AI Tools

Effective: {{policy_effective}}. Applies to all courses. Issued by the Office of Academic Affairs.

## What is allowed

You may use AI tools for research and brainstorming unless the instructor says no for that assignment. Each assignment sheet states one of three rules: AI allowed with disclosure, AI allowed for editing only, or no AI use.

## Disclosure

If AI is allowed, add a note at the end of your submission:

AI disclosure: I used [tool name] to [what you did, for example outline ideas, check grammar]. The final text is my own.

Example: AI disclosure: I used Copilot to check grammar in section 2. The final text is my own.

Citations for AI output go in the same note. Keep a copy of prompts for two weeks after grading.

## What counts as a violation

Submitting AI text as your own without disclosure. Uploading exam questions to an AI tool during a closed exam. Using AI when the sheet says no AI use.

## Reports and penalties

Instructors report cases through the portal. First case: zero on the assignment and a warning letter. Second case: fail in the course and a record with Academic Affairs. You may appeal within 10 days of the notice letter.

Questions: academicaffairs@starlight.edu. Full text is also posted on the university website under Policies.
