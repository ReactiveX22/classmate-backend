## What to bring

Bring your Starlight ID. Bring a pen or pencil. Phones must be off and in your bag, not on the desk. Laptops are allowed only for CSE 310, and you must show a blank desktop at check in.

One page of notes is allowed for CSE 201 and CSE 350. No notes for CSE 481. CSE 310 allows the printed schema sheet handed out in week 6.

## Late arrival and conflicts

If you arrive more than 20 minutes late, you sit in the late row and get no extra time. If two exams overlap, file the conflict form at the CSE office, Room 210, by noon the day before the earlier exam.
