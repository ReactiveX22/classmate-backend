# Tuition Payment Guide, Fall {{semester_year}}

Payment due: {{tuition_deadline}}. Late payments add a {{late_fee}} fee and may place a hold on spring registration.

## How to pay

Online: Student Portal, Financial Services, Make a Payment. Card and bank transfer are accepted. Allow one business day for the balance to update.

In person: Bursar Office, Main Admin Building, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday. Bring your Starlight ID.

Payment plan: three installments. Signup closes 5 days before {{tuition_deadline}}. Contact Financial Services to enroll.

## Holds and aid

A hold blocks course adds and transcript requests. The hold lifts within 24 hours after full payment posts.

Financial aid: check Portal, Account Balance, to confirm aid posted. If aid is missing, write to financialaid@starlight.edu before the deadline. Include your ID and award year.

## Receipts

Receipts are under Portal, Payments, History. Save the PDF. Refunds for dropped courses follow the dates in the course catalog, section 4.
