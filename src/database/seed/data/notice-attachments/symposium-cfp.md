# Tech Symposium {{symposium_year}}, Call for Speakers

Event date: {{symposium_date}}. Venue: Student Center Grand Hall. Theme: Building the Future with AI and Machine Learning. Attendance is free. Registration is required for headcount.

## Talk format

Lightning talks are 20 minutes, plus 5 minutes for questions. One speaker per talk, up to two co authors on the slides. Slides must be PDF, 16 by 9.

Suggested topics: LLMs and applications, computer vision and edge AI, ethical AI, cloud native architecture. Demos are allowed but must run offline in the hall.

## How to submit

Submit through the university portal, Events, Tech Symposium. Include title under 12 words, a 150 word abstract, speaker name and major, and any demo needs such as power or table space.

Submission deadline: {{symposium_cfp_deadline}}. Decisions go out within 7 days after the deadline. Selected speakers receive a printed certificate.

## Day plan

Check in opens at 9:00 AM. Talks run 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Lunch tables are in the west lobby. Poster boards are first come, first served.

Contact: symposium@starlight.edu.
