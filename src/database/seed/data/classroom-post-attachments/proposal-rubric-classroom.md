# CSE 481 Project Proposal Rubric (Classroom Edition)

Course: CSE 481 Natural Language Processing and LLMs. Instructor: Professor Sarah Jenkins (jenkins@starlight.edu). Original deadline was {{proposal_original}}. Extended deadline is {{proposal_deadline}} at 11:59 PM. One upload per team to the classroom portal under Assignments, CSE 481 Proposal.

## Team rules

Teams of 3 to 4 students. Solo proposals are not accepted. Interdisciplinary teams are encouraged. Put team number, all member names, and Starlight IDs on page 1. Register your team on the Team formation sheet before {{proposal_deadline}}.

## Length and format

Two full pages, PDF, 11 point font or larger, 1 inch margins. References may go on a third page. Under two full pages loses 10 points automatically.

## Required sections

1. Problem. Two or three sentences on what you will build and who it helps. Name one concrete user group, for example clinicians triaging discharge notes.
2. Data. Name the dataset, size, and license. Examples: SQuAD v2 (150k questions, CC-BY-SA), CNN/DailyMail (300k pairs). If you collect data, state sample count and labeling protocol.
3. Method. Name the baseline model and one improvement. Baseline examples: bert-base-uncased, Llama-3-8B zero-shot, BM25 + MiniLM reranker. State GPU hours needed on lab machines (each team gets 20 GPU hours on A100s).
4. Evaluation. Name two metrics with splits. Examples: EM and F1 on 80/10/10 train/dev/test, faithfulness rated 1-5 by two annotators.
5. Schedule. Three dated milestones before week 12, with owner names.

## Grading (100 points)

Scope and clarity: 40 points. Method and baseline: 30 points. Evaluation plan: 20 points. Schedule and roles: 10 points.

## Late rule

One day late loses 10 points. After two days the portal closes and the submission is marked missing. If you already submitted before {{proposal_original}}, you do not need to resubmit. Extensions beyond {{proposal_deadline}} require a dean's note.
